# Nova Motion Magic

In this website, make all the animation extremely clean and neat. They should have frames. They should not be jumping from one image to another image. Instead, they should be proper animations, like sliding animations, et cetera, and they should have at least 30 frames per second. All the animation should look neat to the eye, and all the animations inside of the preview should also be extremely clean and neat. And they should also not just be stuttering images or flicking from one image to another. Instead, they should also look like animations and actually have a neat and clean sliding animation, closing, opening animation, et cetera, where it looks extremely neat and clean and is appealing, and almost like a video.
